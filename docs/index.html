Zero-Lag Distributed Architecture

How Proctora solves remote proctoring without expensive cloud AI GPU costs or video bandwidth bottlenecks.

01 · IN-HOUSE AI VISION
Local Machine ML Engine
Candidate machine runs a local Python FastAPI engine (`:5001`). Performs MediaPipe 3D head pose, solvePnP Euler angles, hand-over-mouth detection, and YOLOv8 contraband analysis in <30ms without sending heavy video to the cloud.
02 · DIRECT DESK STREAM
Smartphone QR Stream
Smartphone connects directly over Wi-Fi LAN or in-house Ngrok tunnel, delivering 480x360 desk frames directly to the candidate station and cloud hub with minimal latency.
03 · EVENT TELEMETRY
Cloud Relay Backend
Lightweight Render cloud backend ingests structured violation events (`GAZE_AWAY`, `HAND_OVER_MOUTH`, `SPOKEN_AUDIO_DETECTED`, `CONTRABAND`) and 1 FPS thumbnail previews.
04 · REAL-TIME OVERSIGHT
Invigilator Watch Floor
Hosted on Vercel (`proctora-admin-two.vercel.app`). Dynamic risk scoring algorithm sorts desks by urgency (High → Med → Low) and allows proctors to review incidents or terminate fraudulent sessions.

Quick Start Walkthrough

How a candidate takes an exam and how an invigilator monitors the floor.

1

Download & Launch Kiosk

Install the Proctora Student app for macOS or Windows. Launching the app automatically tests the local vision AI and camera permissions.

2

Seat Check-In & Dual Camera Pairing

The candidate captures a reference face snapshot, speaks a 5-second verification phrase, and scans the QR code on their smartphone to activate the side desk camera.

3

Lockdown & Live Monitoring

Click Begin Exam. The desktop enters full-screen kiosk lock. Invigilators can oversee real-time metrics on the Invigilator Hub.
